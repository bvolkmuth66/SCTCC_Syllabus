About this editor

Nothing you do here changes anyone’s syllabus. You edit a copy of your section as plain text. When you send it, your change goes by email to the reviewer, the syllabus builder reviewer, who checks it and then publishes it. Until then the current wording stays in place.

You can’t break anything: if you lose your way, choose Start over to bring back the current version.

How to format
  • Each line is its own paragraph. Press Enter for a new paragraph.
  • A line starting with - is a bullet. Four spaces before it make a sub-bullet. 1. starts a numbered list.
  • **words** are bold, *words* are italic. Select words and use the buttons.
  • Links are written [words people click](https://address). Use the Link button.
  • Words in curly brackets, like {Withdrawal date}, are filled in from each instructor’s syllabus. Keep them as they are.
